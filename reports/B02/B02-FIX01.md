## 1. Purpose

B02 shipped the exact domain primitives, but the original report left two
acceptance items unverified because the local Windows GNU toolchain lacked the
LLVM coverage profiler runtime and could not build native libFuzzer:

- 100% branch coverage on the financial math;
- native execution of the decimal and instrument fuzz targets.

B02-FIX01 closes those gaps without changing the approved architecture, public
financial interfaces, or financial invariants. It adds a Linux branch-coverage
job, a Linux-native bounded libFuzzer job, reproducible boundary tests, and the
CI fixes needed to make those jobs trustworthy.

### 6.3 Boundary tests added for reachable branches

The coverage result is supported by meaningful boundary tests rather than by
excluding reachable branches:

- Decimal magnitude overflow at `i128::MAX + 1` and below `i128::MIN`.
- Decimal padding overflow when a full-width `i128` is scaled by 18.
- The entire valid `i128` range still round-trips.
- Conversion overflow at each intermediate `price * quantity` product.
- Margin-tier derivation overflow at the final `i128` boundary.
- Invalid zero `tick_size` and `lot_size` during decimal formatting.
- Symbol characters covering digits, hyphens, and underscores.
- Rounding behavior for half-up, up, and down, with the unreachable checked
  arms removed after proof of non-failure.

Final measurements on the Windows host:

run-to-run variance are plausible contributors; the exact cause was not
isolated. The gate is reported as PASS on the executed evidence, and the
narrower margin is recorded as a performance risk in section 13. The
informational benchmarks are not production SLOs.

## 12. Architecture and security compliance

- The approved B00 architecture is unchanged.
- No public financial type, method, error code, or wire contract was removed or
  given different semantics.
- `Scale::factor_i128` keeps its public signature and is now `const`; the new

## 13. Limitations and risks

Carried forward:

1. Docker and Compose remain uninstalled. Image builds, service networking,
   database initialization, broker, cache, object-store, and monitoring runtime
   checks were not performed.
2. GNU Make remains unavailable. The Makefile was not executed as a whole.
3. OTLP trace delivery was not observed end-to-end because no collector is
   running.
4. Ten npm advisories remain in documentation and contract tooling (3 low, 1
   moderate, 6 high). They are below the CI critical threshold; no critical
   advisory is known.
5. Branch protection and signed commits are hosting-platform settings and are
   not enforced by this repository content.
6. `Instrument::tick_size` and `lot_size` remain atomic-unit fields. B04 must
   load configuration in the same units or conversions will be wrong by asset
   scale.
7. Margin tiers that would derive a non-integral maintenance amount are still
   rejected by design; configuration must use compatible boundaries.

New or updated:

1. The hot-conversion benchmark still passes, but its measured median rose from
   33.089 ns in B02 to 43.708 ns in the worst of three B02-FIX01 runs. The
   exact cause was not isolated, and the margin under the 50 ns budget is
   narrower. This should be re-measured on idle production-class hardware
   before being treated as an SLO.
2. The coverage and fuzz jobs are pinned to nightly `nightly-2026-10-01`,
   `cargo-llvm-cov 0.9.1`, and `cargo-fuzz 0.13.2`. Future toolchain upgrades
   must revalidate the JSON parser and fuzz harness.
3. The executed fuzz evidence is a bounded 30-second smoke campaign per
   target. Longer campaigns are documented but were not run in this session.
4. The one uncovered measured branch is in the informational `error.rs` test
   assertion path; it is outside the financial-math gate and is recorded, not
   hidden.
